# Nibbo: a $249 legged pet robot that also watches your home

**Live 3D viewer:** https://wjb000.github.io/nibbo-pawbot/

Nibbo is a palm-sized, four-legged pet robot, 178 × 114 × 133 mm and about 0.49 kg. It trots over when you get home, leans into your hand when you pet its back, shows its mood on a 1.47" face, and walks back to its own bed to nap and charge. It plugs in with its **tail**.

On the useful side, its 160° camera, mic and speaker make it a roaming home check-in. Ask it to "go look at the stove", get person and pet alerts, and have it walk over to deliver reminders.

## Repo

```
site/                  GitHub Pages 3D viewer (three.js): layers, labels, X-ray, explode, views, click-for-info
cad/nibbo.py           parametric CadQuery model of every molded part
cad/elec.py            bought parts (servos, battery, board, display, camera, sensors) placed in the assembly
cad/export.py          builds STEP, ASCII STL, the viewer glTF and the reports (mass, interference)
cad/step/              STEP per molded part (assembly position)
cad/stl/               ASCII STL for prototype printing (print-oriented)
cad/reports/           mass properties, interference check (0 overlaps > 1 mm3 at the nominal pose)
docs/TORQUE.md         actuator torque reasoning
docs/PRODUCTION.md     DFM, tooling, assembly steps, prototype print guide
docs/bom_data.py       single source for BOM.md and the site's BOM panel
```

Rebuild everything with:

```
pip install cadquery trimesh manifold3d fast-simplification
python cad/export.py      # writes cad/step, cad/stl, cad/reports, site/assets/nibbo.gltf
python docs/bom_data.py   # writes BOM.md, site/assets/bom.json
```

The full assembly STEP (`cad/step/nibbo_assembly.step`, about 8 MB) is generated by `export.py`.

## Status: honest gaps

- **Not built or tested.** This is a CAD design study. No part has been printed or fit-checked, and no servo has been load-tested.
- **Knee linkage.** It is a near-parallelogram 5-bar. The viewer's walk preview moves the pushrod with the thigh and does not solve the linkage, and the leg's range of motion has not been swept for collisions yet.
- **Docking.** The tail-to-bed contact geometry is conceptual. The contact height assumes a "sit" pose when docked, and that has not been checked against the leg kinematics.
- **No walking gait yet.** There is no gait, firmware or app, and there is no sim model (URDF/MJCF) yet, although link masses come from this CAD.
- **Prices.** Several volume prices are engineering estimates (marked *est* in BOM.md), most importantly the servo at $2.90. Tooling, certification and the app are not in the per-unit BOM.
- **Wi-Fi antenna.** The ESP32 antenna sits between metal-geared servos. Range needs an RF check, and the U.FL module variant is the fallback.
